Wft Basis Oefenexamen

Practice questions for the Dutch Wft Basis exam

Wft Basis Oefenexamen is an independent practice app for iPhone and iPad to prepare for the Dutch Wft Basis exam. Practise by topic, take a timed practice exam, and see an explanation and source for every question. The questions are in Dutch.

Important: this app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Commissie Deskundigheid Financiële Dienstverlening (CDFD) or DUO. The questions are written in-house and may contain errors. They do not replace the official exam material.
