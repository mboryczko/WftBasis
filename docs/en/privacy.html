Privacy Policy

Wft Basis Oefenexamen · Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: no account is required. Your progress stays on your own device. We only measure anonymously how the app is used, and you can switch that off in Settings. We do not sell data and show no ads.

2. What data do we process?

a. Data that stays on your device

The app stores locally on your iPhone or iPad: your answers and results per question, your exam attempts, your favourites, your chosen language and your settings. This data is not sent to us and we have no access to it. It may be part of a device backup that you make with Apple (iCloud) or via your computer. You can delete it via Settings → Reset progress or by deleting the app.

b. Anonymous usage statistics (TelemetryDeck)

To improve the app we use the analytics service TelemetryDeck, which is designed not to collect personal data. While you use the app, the following is sent:

  • events such as starting the app, starting or finishing a practice session or exam, answering a question (only the category, the exam topic and whether the answer was correct, partially correct or wrong – not your actual answer), showing the purchase screen, purchase events and changing the language;
  • whether you use the free or the premium version and which app language you selected;
  • technical information such as app version, iOS version, device type and language and region settings;
  • a random, hashed user identifier that does not allow us to identify you and is not linked to your name, email address, Apple ID or advertising identifier.

We use this data solely to understand which parts of the app are used, to find bugs, and to improve the app and the questions. According to TelemetryDeck, data is processed on servers within the European Union. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy for more information.

Opt out: go to Settings → Privacy → Anonymous usage statistics and switch it off. No usage statistics will be sent afterwards.

Legal basis: we consider this data anonymous. To the extent it were nonetheless personal data, we process it based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in improving and securing the app. You can object at any time by switching the setting off.

c. Purchases

The one-time premium purchase is handled entirely by Apple (App Store). We receive no payment details, name or email address of buyers. The app checks on your device via StoreKit whether you own the purchase. Apple only provides us with aggregated sales and financial reports. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's processing.

d. Contact and error reports

If you email us (for example to report an error in a question), we process your email address and message to reply and to improve the question. Legal basis: legitimate interest and, for purchase-related questions, performance of a contract. We keep correspondence no longer than necessary to handle your request.

3. What we do not do

  • We show no ads and do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent data to third parties.
  • We do not request access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Sharing with third parties

We only share data with the processors needed to run the app: TelemetryDeck (anonymous usage statistics) and Apple (distribution and payment via the App Store). Where required, we enter into agreements with providers that process data on our behalf in accordance with the GDPR.

5. Retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Usage statistics are retained by TelemetryDeck according to its policy and cannot be traced back to you. We keep correspondence no longer than necessary.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Because we cannot identify you from the anonymous statistics, we cannot link them to you; you can always switch their transmission off. For correspondence you can exercise your rights via mb.software.studio@gmail.com. We respond within one month.

If you have a complaint, you can contact us or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As we are established in Poland, that is the Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO, uodo.gov.pl). You may also contact the data protection authority in your own country, for example the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Security

Data is sent to TelemetryDeck over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Your local data is protected by iOS security features.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date. For significant changes we will also notify you in the app.

10. Contact

Privacy questions? Email mb.software.studio@gmail.com.
