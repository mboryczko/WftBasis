Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app and go to Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple account you used to buy Premium.

I want a refund.

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and select the purchase.

How do I report an error in a question?

Use Settings → Report an error in a question or email us. Please include the question ID (for example Q-0123) and what you think is wrong, with a source if you have one.

Can I change the app language?

Yes: Settings → App language (Nederlands or English). Questions and explanations always stay in Dutch, just like the official exam.

How do I delete my data?

Choose Settings → Reset progress or delete the app. Your progress is stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I switch off statistics?

Settings → Privacy → Anonymous usage statistics.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. All questions are written in-house based on the public exam terms and legislation. The app is not affiliated with CDFD or DUO.
